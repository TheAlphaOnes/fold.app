Fold is a journal you don't have to keep up with. One orange key, three ways to keep a moment:

• Tap to write a line.
• Hold to talk, then tap to stop.
• Swipe up to take a photo.

That's the whole habit. A second to keep it, and it's there whenever you want to find it again.

MAKE A DAY LOOK THE WAY IT FELT
Turn any memory into a canvas. Drop on photos, videos, GIFs, a song, a voice note and the place you were, then move them anywhere. Pick a font and an ink colour for the words.

GATHER MOMENTS INTO STORIES
A trip, a new city, a year of small firsts. File memories into stories and play them back later.

A RHYTHM, NOT A RITUAL
Three gentle nudges a day, one each morning, afternoon and evening, asking for one frame of what you're doing. Your Continuum keeps count of the days in a row and the ink you earn this month, and your profile fills in a year of days at a glance.

FIND IT AGAIN
Scroll your timeline day by day, or open the Time Machine to land on a day from months ago.

YOURS, COMPLETELY
• No account. Open it and start.
• Your memories stay on your phone. We don't upload, sync or back them up, and we can't read them.
• Lock Fold with your fingerprint or face, and hide it from the app switcher and screenshots.
• Deleted memories wait 30 days in Recently Deleted, in case you change your mind.

Fold collects anonymous usage data, such as which features are used and crash reports, to keep the app working. It never includes what you write or capture.
