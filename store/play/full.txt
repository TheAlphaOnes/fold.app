Journaling, but cool.

Fold is the journaling app for Gen Z. Tap to write. Hold to talk. Swipe up to snap. If you can send a text, you can keep a journal. No blank page, no prompts, no pressure.

PHOTO DUMPS, VOICE NOTES, 2AM THOUGHTS. IT ALL COUNTS.
For the overthinkers, the photo dumpers, the late-night voice-noters, and everyone who bought a journal and never opened it. You're in the right place.

TAP. HOLD. SWIPE. THAT'S LITERALLY IT.
• Tap the orange key to write a line.
• Hold it to talk, then tap to stop.
• Swipe it up to snap a photo.

ROMANTICIZE YOUR DAY. LITERALLY.
Throw on photos, videos, songs, GIFs and voice notes, then drag them anywhere. Pick a font and an ink colour for your words. Scrapbook energy, zero glue sticks.

STREAKS THAT HYPE YOU UP, NOT GUILT-TRIP YOU.
Three nudges a day, one each morning, afternoon and evening. A streak that counts, and ink that fills up your month. Miss a day? You're still in.

BETTER THAN A CAMERA ROLL.
Gather memories into stories, watch your year fill in at a glance, and open the Time Machine to land on any day again.

NOT A FEED. NOT A FINSTA. JUST YOURS.
• No account. Open it and go. No sign-up, no email, no password.
• No cloud. Your words, pics and voice notes stay on your phone. We can't see them. Literally can't.
• No ads. Not now, not ever. You're not the product, and your memories aren't inventory.
• Lock it with your fingerprint or face, and hide it from screenshots and the app switcher.
